# Reference-frame study images

These eight JPEGs are **actual still frames** sampled from the user-supplied compressed excerpt of [“Can 2 People Really Restart Humanity?” by Awaken](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6oZ9fdM0n50). They are included for visual study, not as original project artwork or editable character/background assets.

[Visual analysis](../references/visual-reference.md) · [Original examples](../assets/README.md) · [Skill instructions](../SKILL.md) · [Project README](../../../../README.md)

## What the frames show

The images were scaled from 1920 × 1080 to 1280 × 720; filenames indicate approximate times in the supplied excerpt.

| Frame | What to study |
|---|---|
| [00:02 — street character](00-02-street-character.jpg) | Side-view silhouette, thin limbs, hand-to-face gesture, warm window against a cool street. |
| [00:18 — wide city](00-18-city-wide.jpg) | Staged street environment and placement of figures. |
| [00:40 — classroom](00-40-classroom.jpg) | Full-body characters, large board, facial details, warm room, vignette, and pools of light. |
| [01:22 — family diagram](01-22-board-family-diagram.jpg) | Chalklike diagrams on a large board and limited overlays. |
| [02:18 — graphics stage](02-18-genetics-stage.jpg) | Small character at the left, large navy grid field, limited colors, and source line. |
| [02:55 — portrait gallery](02-55-history-gallery.jpg) | Simplified portraits, foreground figure, and warm theatrical setting. |
| [03:22 — village](03-22-village.jpg) | Side-on village tableau, repeated small figures, and visual continuity. |
| [03:36 — prop metaphor](03-36-toolbox-metaphor.jpg) | Large central prop, uncluttered space, and soft directional light. |

The supplied excerpt ends at **3:57.9** of the 6:58 original. It was sampled visually; four selected one-second sequences were examined at 30 fps. The ending was not visually reviewed, and the audio was not auditioned. These frames do not reveal the original rig, software, exact motion, or soundtrack. See the [visual analysis](../references/visual-reference.md) for observations and limits.
